Assignment II: Functional Writing
Suggested time: approximately 40 minutes Suggested word count range: 250 to 500 words Read the situation below and complete the assignment that follows. Situation
You are Peyton Tanner, a Grade 9 student who lives in Korgan, Alberta. You recognize that volunteering is an important part of personal growth that is beneficial to both those who volunteer and those who receive the service. As a result, you have created a Student Volunteer Club at Canyon Heights School in Korgan, and you have recruited many students to join this club. Some of the club’s activities include snow shovelling and lawn mowing for senior citizens, organizing peer⁠-⁠led tutorial sessions, and coordinating after‑school activities for younger students. Even though there are many opportunities for members to donate their time and skills, the individuals in the community who could use the services this club provides may not know it exists. Promotions that reach a large number of community members could increase public awareness of the Student Volunteer Club. You have decided to write a business email to Mr. Nathan Malette, the general manager of local radio station CHIC 101.1 FM in Crimsonville, requesting that the station promote the Student Volunteer Club to the community. In your email, you should highlight the benefits of the services provided by the Student Volunteer Club.
Assignment
Write a business email to Mr. Nathan Malette, general manager of radio station CHIC 101.1 FM, requesting that the station promote the Student Volunteer Club in its broadcasts. Provide enough information to convince Mr. Malette of the benefits of the services provided by the members of the Student Volunteer Club.
When writing, be sure to
  • identify the purpose of the email
  • explain the details of the situation and request
  • organize your thoughts appropriately in sentences and paragraphs
  • use vocabulary that is interesting and effective
